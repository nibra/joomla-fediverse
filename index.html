What it does

Everything you need to federate

A full ActivityPub implementation for Joomla — from WebFinger discovery to outbox delivery.

🌐

ActivityPub Federation

Full server-to-server federation: signed HTTP requests, shared inbox, follower delivery, Like and Announce handling.

📡

WebFinger & NodeInfo

Your actors are discoverable via @user@yoursite.example on any Fediverse app. NodeInfo exposes your instance metadata.

✏️

Article Publishing

Publish Joomla articles as ActivityPub Notes, Articles, Images or Videos. Choose the object type per actor or per article.

❤️

Reactions Module

Show Like and Boost counts on your articles. Optional action buttons let visitors boost or like directly from your site.

🔖

Shortcode Tags

Embed {{fediverse:follow}}, {{fediverse:handle}} and {{fediverse:reactions}} anywhere in your content.

🛡️

Domain Policies Pro

Allow or block entire domains. Keep trolls and spam instances away from your actors' inboxes.

🔑

OAuth 2.0 / C2S Pro

Client-to-Server ActivityPub support. Use Mastodon-compatible apps to post and interact as your Joomla actor.

⚙️

Scheduled Tasks

Joomla's task scheduler handles outbox delivery, inbox processing, key rotation and queue cleanup automatically.

🔄

Key Rotation Pro

Rotate HTTP Signature key pairs on a schedule. Old keys are re-announced to followers automatically.
